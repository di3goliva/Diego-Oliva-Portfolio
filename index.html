Blood pressure data acquisition system

Analog conditioning with an instrumentation amplifier and Sallen-Key bandpass filters isolates microvolt oscillometric signals. A Python algorithm using periodograms and dynamic peak detection extracts MAP, systolic, and diastolic pressure while rejecting artifacts.

Try the demo: add noise, then switch the filter on to watch the signal come back.

  • Python
  • Analog circuits
  • Signal processing
The dim line is a raw noisy reading, the bright line is the filtered signal. A simplified demo, not your real data.

Microfluidic gradient generator

Designed a PDMS device for neutrophil chemotaxis and modeled transport to verify laminar flow (Re < 1) and a stable gradient. Fabricated with SU-8 photolithography and validated with fluorescence microscopy and ImageJ tracking.
